Add it all up and you realize that [Impostor Syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impostor_syndrome) is kind of like a reverse [Dunning Kruger effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunning%E2%80%93Kruger_effect).  Dunning Kruger, by definition, only really affects people below the median.  

On the other hand, Impostor Syndrome is a sickness of the competent.  If you suffer from it, paradoxically, you don't really have anything to worry about.
