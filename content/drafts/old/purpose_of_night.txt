.===== The Purpose of Night =====

Ghosts and banshees, trainwrecks and freefalls\\
Spooky nights and cobwebs and graveyards\\
Demons and devils are only the cause\\
But they are not the fear I shake from\\
The evil men that do good things\\
And the good men who do very evil things\\
The citizens and denizens, the combatants and bombardments\\
I see them and wonder how different I am, but I do not fear them\\
The daytimes are filled with the lackluster feel\\
Of neutral gray walls, the formica halls\\
The daily coffee grinds and pencil pushing\\
The classifications and formulations for someone else's tabulation\\
The nighttimes are bright blue seas of stars, eternal and content\\
Foggy, damp meadows, soft whistling cries\\
Clouds and trees framing the sky, looking to heaven\\
and I remember what its like to be human.\\
My fear is not my starkest fright, or my dull dreary daytime fade to death\\
Because we don't look at our abilities and civilities and upward fiscal motilities\\
Deep down we don't shudder at being incapable of being capable.\\
So I look up at the deep, silent sky, and I fear not knowing who I am.\\

=== Notes ===

I wrote this one night, sitting outside at about 1 AM.  You forget how alive the night is.  It's not really refined, and it seems to sound better spoken than read, but, well, here it is.
