I lived in Columbia for five years.  It was an adequate place to live.  I would not call it a great place to live.  It fixed oft-cited community deficincies like crime rates but only ever strived for a simple middle class mediocrity.  For this, it is highly touted in magazine	s and newspapers as one of the best places in the US to live.

Now that I've moved out of Columbia, I consider it a failed community built on misguided precepts.  

I saw some of this while I lived there, but only after moving has it been truly apparent.





The intent was to provide a scale of low to high income earners and put them all next to each other.  But this is a failure, and it has to be.  The rich can always buy exclusivity.  At best, you get high middle class to middle poor.
