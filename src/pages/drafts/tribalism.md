Jordan Peterson has gotten one thing right, and he's irritated many people based on his perspective.  A lot of people hide their dislike for Peterson behind claims of transphobia, racism,  fascism (which is especially weird), and lots more.

But at the end of the day, despite any of his more specific views that I do or don't agree with, it's this one view that's pissed so many people off.

And to describe that one view, I'm going to turn to Russ Roberts, who recently wrote a [fantastic Medium article](https://medium.com/@russroberts/i-cant-hear-you-e7a218831f07) extolling the many concerns and issues around this problem.

Roberts, like many of us, has growing concerns about the temperature of political discourse in our country.
