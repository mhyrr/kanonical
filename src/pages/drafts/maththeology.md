Think for a second about the most beautiful ideas you've ever run across.  This is not usually a characteristic you use to group ideas.  I know I hadn't done it before, until I ran across [this delightful little blog entry](http://unschooled.org/2011/11/the-most-beautiful-ideas-ive-encountered/).

The list of ideas here is fantastic.  And a good number of them would be on my list too.  The ones that aren't are only because I haven't delved into them as much as I should.  But some of them - like the different types of infinity, and Decidability - when I learned them, they moved me on a fundamental and spiritual level.

Which is another striking thing to say.  Notions of decidability and infinity are mathematical, what's spirituality got to do with it?

Examples: decidability, infinity, turing machine, evolution

Math and theology, math as the best complementary subject, so that you learn probability, infinity, Godel, etc. this gets bad mouthed for being a direct comparison, but used as a mode of thinking it's fantastic.

Speaking/thinking with precision

Knowing math solves two problems with religion.

1. It solves the problem of quacks.  If fundamentalists of any sort learn math at this level, it will (I think) be enough to shake the core of their identity and break it away from the myths they attach themselves to.

2. It informs the educated religious in an important and complementary way.  Christianity in particular focuses on compassion and a personal encounter with Christ.  It is intimate.  It has many manifestations of this intimacy, for example St. Therese of Liseux's Way of the Little Flower.  But it often lacks a larger aspect(I tried to formulate this in We Need More Scientist Saints).  Religious people don't often deal with probabilities, with populations, or with trends.  But there's a lot of good to be done in the world with this line of thinking.  It's also impossibly hard.. the realm of unintended consequences is very large.  So knowing the types of problems and the method of thinking is hugely important.
