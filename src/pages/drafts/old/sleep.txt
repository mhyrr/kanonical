====== Reminiscence ======

Waking from a dream\\
I sought my sleep remitted\\
To see once more the girl\\
Whom fate had to me fitted\\


And tossing in a sweat\\
I fought my wakeful eyes\\
To capture again my sleep\\
And see my lover's guise\\


==== Notes ====

I always felt this was incomplete, and it may very well be, but after reading many other selections, short lyric verse is just another form - not necessarily incomplete.
