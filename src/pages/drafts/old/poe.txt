====== A Hymn ======

I discovered this quite by accident while reading tonight.  It's a beautiful little poem with simple patterns and simple language, but some of the very best thoughts tend to cling to such a structure.  It also happens to come from a fairly unusual source - Edgar Allan Poe - typically known for his macabre, spectral works.

Simply titled: "Hymn"

At morn- at noon- at twilight dim-\\
Maria! thou hast heard my hymn!\\
In joy and woe- in good and ill\\
Mother of God, be with me still!\\
When the Hours flew brightly by,\\
And not a cloud obscured the sky,\\
My soul, lest it should truant be,\\
Thy grace did guide to thine and thee;\\
Now, when storms of Fate o'ercast\\
Darkly my Present and my Past,\\
Let my Future radiant shine\\
With sweet hopes of thee and thine!\\
