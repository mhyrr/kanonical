Need to edit Scientist Saint first..

Money is what makes the world go aroudn - for better or worse.  When I was a kid I was always disappointed by the idea of "Charity" as I understood it.  I give this money away and.. what happens?  I feel no benefit, I see no need assuaged.  I am entirely decoupled beyond the process of forking it over.  And I'm supposed to feel good about this?  This described the notion of Love?  I don't think so.. there's more to it..

The example of the technophilanthropist.  What does he do?  He doesn't just tithe his money away, that's insufficient and linear.  Instead, he tries to create opportunities that are exponential, and can help many more people.

The notion of tithing is linear.  It helps only the local church, maybe the local diocese, minimally the entire Church.  There's a huge need for the dreamers - the economists that can make a buck go much further for the needs of humanity.  The Church is still suck in the linear notion of money - we need to be exponential.
