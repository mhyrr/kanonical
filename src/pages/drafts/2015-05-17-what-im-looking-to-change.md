I've been looking for a change lately, and to figure out what the next step is, it seems important to figure out what's missing first.  Here's an attempt at a list:

- Work that matters.  Too much of the work I do now is BS and irrelevant.  I'd rather focus on what matters.
- Time with family and friends.  I work too much.  Or rather, I put in too many hours.
- Autonomy.  I have a lot of it, but I want more.  Autonomy means setting my own schedule and controlling the time in front of me.
- The future.  I know I want to be doing something different in the future.  The first shot at this was looking 10 years out.  I'm 34 now - do I want to be where I am now at 44?  The answer is no.  I want to be spending less time working, more time writing or enjoying my family and focusing on what matters.

These all sound good, but how realistic can I be?  The money from a job is huge.  Well, I think I would be happier with half the money and more of the time.

Time to set goals:

1.  My own company, software based, less than 10 people
2.  32 hour work weeks
3.  As flat as possible, complete autonomy and trust for everyone.  If you don't feel they can have this, then they shouldn't be hired.
4.  Focus on product and support, and really not much else.
5.  Flat monthly fees for recurring incomes.

What is stopping me?

Fear (failure, being wrong, leaving something "good")
Laziness
