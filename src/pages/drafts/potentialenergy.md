My last goal of 2018 was to [focus on doing](/2018-baller/), and to revive the joy of doing, without regard for the outcome.  I've been trying to understand for myself exactly what this means.  It's been a struggle to formulate it properly - it's kind of complex - but I think I finally have a metaphor for it that fits.

The metaphor is potential and kinetic energy.  Potential energy can be dangerous, while kinetic energy can be fruitful.  I want to focus on kinetic energy.

If you remember your basic science classes, potential energy is the kind of energy that is stored in an object based on position, stress or other things.  A ball at the top of a hill has potential energy.  So does a compressed spring, or a taut crossbow.  They're ready to unleash.

On the other hand, kinetic energy is in motion.  The ball rolling down the hill.  The compressed spring bouncing out.  The crossbow firing it's bolt.  It is active and working, literally.  The physics definition of work is force over distance.

That's where I want to be, working.
